Process Notes

Reflection

In this topic I learnt how to edit images and make them look more professional. I practised cropping, resizing, and improving the overall appearance of pictures. What worked well was experimenting with different tools and seeing how small changes could make a big difference.

One challenge I had was choosing the best colours and effects without making the design look messy. Next time, I would spend more time planning my design before editing so the final result is even stronger.
